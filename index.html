01 / About

Learning how to turn ideas into working systems.

I’m a Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, working at the intersection of computational logic, embedded electronics and mechanical control systems. I build physical things, and my builds have won contests, such as ROVIE.

My current toolkit is C (pure and embedded), embedded C++, MATLAB, and CAD. I approach engineering through first principles and systems thinking: I like understanding how things work, then turning that understanding into prototypes that are useful, functional, and actually work.

I want to work in robotics, whether in research or hands-on, building the hardware and writing the software that brings robots to life. I’m open to collaborations, projects, and roles in robotics and embedded systems. Feel free to get in touch.

02 Robotics · Firmware

Autonomous Obstacle-Avoiding Robot

A four-wheel-drive rover that follows lines and avoids obstacles. Built on an ESP32, it uses dual TCRT5000 IR sensors for line detection, an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor for distance sensing, and an L298N driver to control four DC motors.

ESP32 · HC-SR04 · TCRT5000 · L298N · DC Motors · C++
