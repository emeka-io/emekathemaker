About

I am an undergraduate Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, passionate about industrial automation, control engineering, and bringing hardware to life.

My workflow spans designing electronic circuit schematics in KiCad, writing low-level firmware in C/C++, developing autonomous logic with ROS 2 and Python, and validating physical systems using MATLAB and Simulink. I believe in building tools that bridge the digital and physical worlds.

Languages C, C++, Python, Bash
Tools ROS 2, MATLAB/Simulink, KiCad, Onshape
Hardware Arduino, ESP32, STM32, Sensors
