About Me

I am an undergraduate Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, passionate about industrial automation, control engineering, and bringing hardware to life.

My workflow spans designing electronic circuit schematics in KiCad, writing low-level firmware in C/C++, developing autonomous logic with ROS 2 and Python, and validating physical systems using MATLAB and Simulink. I believe in building tools that bridge the digital and physical worlds while documenting the engineering journey.

Genesis Environment Monitor

A standalone environmental sensing product evolving from breadboard prototypes. Features DHT11 integration, an RGB LED status interface, and non-blocking timing logic displayed on a 1.3-inch SH1106 OLED.
