01 / About

Learning how to turn ideas into machines.

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, interested in the systems that connect computation, electronics, mechanics, and control.

My work starts with small embedded systems and gradually moves toward more capable machines — from sensors and microcontrollers to robotics, automation, and autonomous systems.

I care about understanding how things actually work, building physical prototypes, and documenting the process rather than simply producing something that looks impressive.
